Portugal is the home of cork. Around half of the world's cork comes from Portuguese forests, and the cork oak (*Quercus suber*) has been the country's national tree since 2011. For anyone looking for authentic cork furniture, lighting or decorative art, Portugal is where the material, the know-how and the craft all meet.

At Sildel, we have worked solely and exclusively with cork for more than forty years. From our atelier in Esmoriz, on the coast just south of Porto, we turn genuine Portuguese cork into one-of-a-kind pieces for homes, hotels and design studios. This article explains what makes Portuguese cork different, and what to look for when you choose a piece made from it.

## Why Portuguese cork is considered the finest in the world

**A protected forest, harvested by hand.** Cork comes from the bark of the cork oak. The tree is never cut down: skilled harvesters strip the outer bark by hand, and the tree grows a new layer. A cork oak is first harvested when it is around 25 years old, and then only every nine years. In Portugal the cork oak is protected by law, so the forests that supply our material are managed for the long term.

**The montado, a living ecosystem.** Portuguese cork forests, the *montado*, are among the richest habitats in Europe. They shelter hundreds of plant species, birds of prey and the endangered Iberian lynx, the animal that inspired the Sildel emblem. Choosing cork helps keep these forests valuable, and therefore protected.

**Generations of expertise.** Portugal has the world's largest cork industry. That depth of knowledge, from the harvest to the selection of each plank, is what allows an atelier like ours to work with the most expressive pieces of bark, including the rough outer layer that most industries discard.

## Genuine cork versus cork-look materials

As cork has become fashionable in interior design, many products now use the word loosely. Here is how to recognise the real thing:

- **Natural, irregular texture.** Genuine cork bark carries fissures, grain and scars from the tree it came from. No two pieces are identical, which is why every Sildel treasure is unique.
- **Lightness and warmth.** Real cork is remarkably light and warm to the touch, because it is made of millions of tiny air-filled cells.
- **Natural resistance.** Cork is naturally resistant to moisture and wear, and it ages beautifully rather than looking tired.
- **Clear origin.** A serious maker can tell you where the cork comes from and who shaped it. Ours is Portuguese, and every piece is handcrafted in Esmoriz.

## From forest to treasure: how a Sildel piece is made

1. **Selection.** We choose bark for its character: a dramatic fissure, a deep colour, an unusual curve.
2. **Shaping by hand.** Our artisans follow the natural form of the bark instead of forcing it into a mould, so the piece keeps the memory of the tree.
3. **Pairing with noble materials.** Many pieces combine cork with Carrara marble, solid wood, glass, metal or porcelain, created with Portuguese partners.
4. **Finishing and packing.** Each treasure is finished, checked and packed by the same hands that made it, ready to travel anywhere in the world.
