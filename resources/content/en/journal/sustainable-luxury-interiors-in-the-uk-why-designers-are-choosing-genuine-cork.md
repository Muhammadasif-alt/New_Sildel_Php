In the United Kingdom, sustainable luxury is no longer a niche. From London townhouses to country homes in the Cotswolds and boutique hotels in Edinburgh, clients want interiors that feel rich and characterful, but also responsible. Genuine cork from Portugal has become one of the most interesting answers to that brief.

Here is why UK interior designers are specifying cork, how it suits British homes, and how to source authentic handcrafted cork pieces from Portugal.

## Cork and the British love of character

British interiors have always valued patina, craftsmanship and objects with history: an inherited table, a worn leather chair, a piece of studio pottery. Handcrafted cork belongs to that tradition. Each Sildel piece keeps the fissures and grain of the cork oak it came from, so it looks collected rather than bought, and it grows more beautiful with time.

## Why cork is a sustainable luxury material

- **No trees are cut down.** Cork is the bark of the cork oak. It is stripped by hand, and the tree grows a new layer, ready to be harvested again nine years later.
- **Protected forests.** Portuguese cork forests are protected by law and support remarkable biodiversity, including the endangered Iberian lynx.
- **Natural, renewable, recyclable.** Cork needs no synthetic finish to look beautiful and can be recycled at the end of its life.
- **Made to last.** A genuine cork piece is designed to be kept for decades, the opposite of fast furniture.

## How cork suits British homes

**Warmth for northern light.** The soft, cool daylight of a British winter can make rooms feel flat. Cork's warm tones and textured surface bring depth, especially under the gentle glow of cork lighting such as Crescent or Leaf.

**A natural partner for period architecture.** In Georgian and Victorian rooms, with cornicing, panelling and marble fireplaces, a sculptural cork bowl or mirror creates a striking contrast between the classical and the organic. Our Crescent suspension, for example, looks remarkable beneath high ceilings and beside gilded mirrors.

**Calm in contemporary spaces.** In modern extensions and minimalist flats, cork adds texture without clutter, balancing polished concrete, oak floors and large glazing.
