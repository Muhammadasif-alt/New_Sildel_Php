After a certain age, most of us already have everything we need. That is exactly why choosing a gift becomes harder, and why the best gifts are the ones that carry meaning: something made by hand, with a story behind it, that will be used and remembered. A piece of genuine Portuguese cork is that kind of gift.

## What makes a gift meaningful

A meaningful gift usually has three qualities. It is made with care, often by hand. It has a story the person can tell when a guest asks about it. And it lasts, so it becomes part of the home rather than something put away after the occasion.

Each Sildel piece is shaped by hand in our atelier in Esmoriz, Portugal, from the bark of the cork oak. The tree is never cut down: the bark is harvested by hand and grows back for the next harvest, about nine years later. It is a gift that is beautiful, useful and respectful of nature, which matters to many people today.

## Gift ideas for life's milestones

### Wedding anniversaries

For a couple who love to entertain, the [Bonfire](/treasures/bonfire) candle holder makes every dinner feel like a celebration. For a quieter couple, a [Leaf](/treasures/leaf-golden) table lamp brings soft light to the evenings they spend together.

### Retirement

Retirement is the time for reading, gardening and long afternoons at home. A small [Eclipse](/treasures/eclipse-silver) lamp beside a favourite chair, or the [Olympia](/treasures/olympia) side table for books and a cup of tea, are gifts that will be used every day.

### A child's or grandchild's wedding

A piece for their first home becomes part of the family story. A cork bowl such as [Shell](/treasures/shell) or a [Halley](/treasures/halley) mirror is something they can keep for decades, and perhaps pass on one day.

### Christenings, first communions and religious anniversaries

Candlelight belongs to these occasions. The [Vitavele](/treasures/vitavele) candle holder, a long strip of natural cork with a row of glass cups, is a gift for a family table or a home altar that will be lit at every celebration to come.

### For the friend who loves flowers

[Vitaqua](/treasures/vitaqua) holds single stems in glass tubes set into natural cork bark. It turns a few flowers from the garden, or a small dried bouquet, into a centrepiece.

Sometimes the right gift does not exist yet. Tell us who the gift is for and what the occasion is, and we will reply personally with what is possible.

## How ordering works

Our pieces are sold by personal enquiry, so you speak to a person, not a checkout page:

1. **Write to us** about the piece and the occasion, and tell us when the gift is needed.
2. **We reply personally** with the price, the delivery time and any questions.
3. **Before dispatch, we send you photographs** of the actual piece, because no two are alike.
4. **We pack it by hand** and deliver it worldwide by DHL. Postage depends on the destination.

If the date matters, a birthday, a wedding or Christmas, write to us early so there is time to choose calmly.

[Write to our atelier](/contact#atelier) and tell us about the person you are thinking of. We will gladly help you find the right piece.
