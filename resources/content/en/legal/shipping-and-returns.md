## Shipping

Shipments are made by DHL to all over the world. The calculation of postage is done automatically, depending on the country and products. After payment, the product is packed and prepared, and shipped after 4 working days. The delivery time depends on each country. You will receive this information when you calculate the shipping costs.

## Returns

### Exchange for other articles

An exchange can be made within 14 days (continuous) after receipt of the item. The item must keep the identification tag (the whole exchange process must be completed within 14 days). The exchange will be made by the carrier subcontracted by Sildel that delivers the new item and picks up the old one (the customer bears all costs inherent to the return and its new shipment).

### Exchange and returns of defective products

The customer is entitled to an exchange or refund if the products supplied are found to be defective, not the product chosen, or other non-conformities with the items ordered. The exchange can be made within 14 days (consecutive) after receiving the item and the product must keep the identification label (the whole exchange process must be completed within 14 days). If it is confirmed that the product does not comply with the order placed, the exchange will be made at no cost to the customer. The customer can choose to exchange or refund the total amount of the purchase (article + shipping) provided that they send by email an invoice with associated costs.

### Reimbursement

The return can be made within 14 (consecutive) days after receiving the item and the product must keep the identification label (the entire return process must be completed within 14 days). The customer can choose to refund the value of the item; the shipping costs will be the responsibility of the customer. The amount for the item will always be paid by bank transfer after our team has received and analysed the product (it must keep its labels and be without signs of use), informing the customer of the refund.

## How to return an item

1. **Send an email** to [sildel@sildel.pt](mailto:sildel@sildel.pt) with a photo of the articles you received.
2. **Pack the article.** You can use the original packaging in which you received the order, or another packaging or box, as long as it is undamaged. If you use the original packaging, empty it and place only the item you want to return. Close the box and seal it with tape. If the box has any labels, stickers or other materials from previous shipments, remove them and paste the data that will be provided in response to the email in step 1.
3. **Exchange collection and delivery.** The transport company subcontracted by Sildel (DHL) will go to the indicated address to collect and deliver the new item defined by both parties via email in step 1.
4. **Refund.** After receiving and analysing the product, the seller will inform the customer of the refund to which they are entitled (if applicable). Refund of the amount paid by bank transfer. All shipping addresses, as well as collection or shipping procedures, will be indicated via email from sildel@sildel.pt.

## The right to regret your purchase

*Valid only within the EU, for consumers.*

Voluntary refusal of an order for any reason. You have 14 (consecutive) days after receiving your order at the designated address to contact us and express your will to forfeit the purchase. Sildel will refund the consumer (excluding the initial shipping costs). The refund will be performed using the same means of payment used in the purchase process. However, we may retain the refund until we have recovered the product and verified its pristine condition.
